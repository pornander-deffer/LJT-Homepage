My research focuses on **natural language processing and machine learning**, with interests in:

- **LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning**
- **Hallucination in vision-language models**
- **LLM truthfulness and interpretability**

I work with Professor Junxian He in the HKUST NLP Group. My publications cover logical reasoning data, chart understanding, truthfulness and hallucination in language models, foundation-model evaluation, and parameter-efficient modules.
